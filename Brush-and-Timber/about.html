Meet the Artist

Welcome to Brush & Timber! My work is deeply rooted in a lifelong passion for the outdoors, wildlife, and traditional craftsmanship. Combining a background in environmental conservation with hands-on mediums like woodworking, leathercraft, and fine art painting, every piece is crafted with a focus on heirloom quality and authentic detail.

Whether I'm capturing the unique spirit of a companion animal in a custom pet portrait, hand-painting wildlife onto vintage crosscut saw blades, or commemorating a memorable outdoor harvest, my goal is to create functional and rustic art that tells a story.

Thank you for supporting independent art and traditional craftsmanship. If you have a custom project in mind, I would love to bring your vision to life.
