Meet the Artist

Crafting Art Inspired by Nature

Welcome to Brush & Timber! Every piece of art created here stems from a deep appreciation for wildlife, the outdoors, and hand-crafted traditions.

Whether it's capturing the unique personality of a beloved pet in a custom portrait, painting detailed wildlife scenes onto vintage timber and saws, or building rustic wooden accents, every creation is approached with care and attention to detail.

Thank you for supporting independent, handmade fine art. Feel free to reach out through the contact form on the home page for custom commissions or inquiries!
