Shipping & Policies

Everything you need to know about delivery, local pickup, and orders.

Shipping & Handling

Every piece of original art—especially our hand-painted vintage saw blades—is carefully packed using rigid support boards, heavy-duty cardboard, and multiple layers of protective bubble wrap to ensure it arrives safely.

Processing Time: Orders typically ship within 2 to 3 business days via secure ground shipping with tracking and full insurance included.

Local Pickup

If you are local and would prefer to pick up your art piece directly, select or note local pickup when purchasing, or email us to coordinate a convenient time and location.

Returns & Damaged Items

Because these are one-of-a-kind original artworks, all sales are final.

Transit Damage: If your package arrives damaged from shipping, please save all packing materials and take photos of the box and the item immediately. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery so we can file an insurance claim and make it right.
